Stela is a software development and IT company focused on making reliable, affordable technology useful to businesses, schools and organisations in Zimbabwe.

Why we’re here

Technology should
be useful to everyone.

Many organisations want better digital tools but don’t have the budget or in-house IT team to build and maintain them alone. We believe that shouldn’t keep a growing business from getting dependable software.

We develop subscription-based business systems, custom applications and websites, and we support the wider IT needs that help those systems work well. Our focus is practical: understand the work, choose the right technology and build for the people who will use it.

From Harare, we aim to contribute to Zimbabwe’s digital transformation with solutions that are locally relevant, scalable and built around real operating needs.

OUR MISSION↗

Make dependable technology accessible.

We empower organisations with affordable, intelligent and reliable digital solutions that simplify operations, improve productivity and support sustainable growth.

OUR VISION✳

Help shape Africa’s digital future.

We want to become a trusted African technology company, supporting Zimbabwe’s digital transformation through accessible and sustainable solutions.
