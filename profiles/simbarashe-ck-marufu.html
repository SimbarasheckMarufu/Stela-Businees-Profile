BIOGRAPHY

About Simbarashe

Simbarashe C.K. Marufu is an Information Systems graduate and technology professional with experience across web development, IT support, network configuration, systems administration and data analysis. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce Honours Degree in Information Systems from Midlands State University, graduating with Upper Second Class (2.1) honours.

His professional experience includes supporting ICT operations in public-sector and local-government environments, maintaining computer systems and network connectivity, resolving hardware and software issues, capturing and analysing operational data, and contributing to computer-literacy awareness initiatives. He currently works remotely as a Web Developer with EFSK Technologies, a United States-based company.

His technical toolkit includes Python, JavaScript, Node.js, React, Next.js, HTML, CSS, PostgreSQL, MySQL and MongoDB, complemented by practical knowledge of Linux, Raspberry Pi configuration, LAN/WAN networking, ICT security and business collaboration tools. This combination enables him to approach technology from both the application-development and IT operations perspectives.

His professional vision is to make work lighter and decision-making clearer by turning complex data and everyday business processes into simple, accessible and dependable digital solutions. He is particularly interested in developing applications and support systems that help organisations improve efficiency, understand their data and deliver better services.
