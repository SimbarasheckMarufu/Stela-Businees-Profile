About Zivaishe

Zivaishe Samson Mabeka is an Information Systems graduate, software developer and IT systems professional with experience in enterprise IT support, systems administration, software development, cloud infrastructure and cybersecurity.

He holds a Bachelor of Commerce Honours Degree in Information Systems from Midlands State University, graduating with Upper Second Class honours.

His professional interests include software engineering, cloud computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, data analytics and blockchain technology. As Co-Founder, CEO and Lead Engineer of Stela, he is focused on developing innovative, affordable and scalable technology solutions that empower businesses and organisations to embrace digital transformation.

His vision is to contribute to Zimbabwe's technological advancement by making professional digital solutions accessible to businesses of all sizes.
