What does Stela do?

We build business software and provide IT services. That includes subscription-based systems, custom software, websites and web applications, cybersecurity, analytics, CCTV and computer hardware.

What is SaaS?

SaaS means “Software as a Service.” Instead of buying and maintaining all the software infrastructure yourself, your team accesses the system online, usually through a subscription. Hosting, maintenance and updates are managed centrally.

Which organisations do you work with?

Our solutions are designed for small and medium-sized businesses, schools, retailers, restaurants, property managers, institutions, NGOs and startups. We begin by understanding your needs and whether we’re a good fit.

Can you build software for a specific business process?

Yes. We can discuss custom software, web applications and system integrations based on your requirements. We’ll first clarify the workflow and recommend an approach before defining the work.

Will a website or application work on mobile phones?

We design responsive websites and web applications to adapt to common screen sizes, including phones, tablets and desktop computers. The exact device and browser requirements are discussed for each project.

How do you help protect business systems?

Our cybersecurity services can include security assessments, system hardening, access control, vulnerability management and security consulting. The right scope depends on your systems and requirements.

How much does a project cost?

Cost depends on the work involved, features, integrations and delivery requirements. Send us a brief description through the quote form and we’ll review it before preparing a quotation.

How long will development take?

Timeframes depend on the project scope and requirements. After an initial discussion, we can clarify what is involved and provide a more useful estimate—rather than guessing before we understand the work.

How do I request a quote?

Use our request-a-quote form to describe your organisation, the service you need and the outcome you’re looking for. Our business email destination is still being set up, so the form prepares an email draft for you to review and send once the address is configured.

Do I need to understand IT to work with you?

No. It helps to explain the task you want to improve or the problem you’ve run into. We’ll ask clear questions, explain technical options in everyday language and agree on the next step together.
